HOW TO INSTALL
==============

This is a test build of an Elden Ring mod pack for playing with Seamless Co-op. Everyone in
the same session should install the same zip.

YOU NEED
- Elden Ring on Steam, up to date (the title screen shows "App Ver. 1.17.2" bottom right).
- Seamless Co-op 2.0.3, installed the usual way (you start the game with ersc_launcher.exe).

INSTALL
1. Close Elden Ring.
2. Extract this whole zip to a folder of its own, for example on your Desktop.
   Do not extract it inside the ELDEN RING folder.
3. Double-click  Install_Without_ModEngine.bat
   It finds Elden Ring through Steam and copies the mod into ELDEN RING\Game\OfflineMods.
   If it says it can't find the game, it prints the command to run with your game folder.
4. Start the game the way you always do for co-op: ersc_launcher.exe.

If Windows or your antivirus asks about the .bat file or xinput1_4.dll: they are part of the
mod (the small loader that lets the game read the mod's files). Allow them.

HOW TO TELL IT WORKED
Rest at any Site of Grace. The menu has three new lines: Seeker's Compass, Loadouts and Camp.

WHAT YOU GET
- Map marks: buy the Seeker's Compass from Merchant Kale at the Church of Elleh (100 runes),
  then choose "Seeker's Compass" at a grace to mark weapons, spells, armor and talismans on
  the map by the damage stats you pick.
- Loadouts: at a grace, save your current gear to one of five slots and equip it again later.
- Camp (Grace Items): graces you have found give you items. Equip a seat and two keepsakes
  under Camp, kill enemies, then rest: for a while, the hits you land build stacks that
  make you stronger (more damage, a protective shell, healing, stamina or poise, by keepsake).
  The more you killed before resting, the more stacks you can hold. You glow at full stacks.
- Auto pickup: plants, items lying in the world and your own lost runes are picked up as you
  walk past. Things enemies drop still need the button.
- Merchant bell bearings: walk up to a wandering merchant and you get their bell bearing, so
  the Twin Maiden Husks sell their stock.
- Roundtable hubs: after you beat a story boss, Smithing Master Hewg and the Twin Maiden Husks
  also stand in that boss room. The rooms are listed under Roundtable Hold in the grace list
  on the map. In Roundtable Hold itself the Husks now sit beside Hewg.
- Damage Dial (off unless you turn it on): makes you take less or more damage. It only
  changes the damage YOU take, also in someone else's game.

SETTINGS
ELDEN RING\Game\OfflineMods\SeekersCompass\seekers_compass.ini  (open it with Notepad)
- Each feature has a section with  Enabled = 1  (on) or  0  (off). Restart the game after
  changing Enabled.
- Damage Dial: under [DamageDial] set  Enabled = 1  and  DamageTaken = 50  for half damage
  (100 is normal).
- Auto pickup: under [AutoPickup] choose what gets picked up.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- Send  ELDEN RING\Game\OfflineMods\SeekersCompass\seekers_compass.log  to whoever gave you
  this zip. Its first lines say which features started.
- To switch one feature off, set its  Enabled = 0  in the settings file.

UPDATE
Extract the new zip and run Install_Without_ModEngine.bat again. Your settings, loadouts and
Camp progress are kept.

UNINSTALL
Run  Uninstall_Without_ModEngine.bat  from the extracted folder. Your saves are untouched.
(It also removes your saved loadouts and Camp progress, which are kept in the mod's folder.)

GOOD TO KNOW
- This only runs through Seamless Co-op's launcher or other offline starts. It never touches
  your normal online save and stays off when Easy Anti-Cheat is running.
- If you already have the separate Seeker's Compass, Grace Loadouts or Grace Items mods
  installed this way, the installer replaces them and keeps your loadouts and progress.
- If another mod already uses ELDEN RING\Game\xinput1_4.dll, the installer stops and tells you.
