WHAT THE MOD DOES
=================

A test build (2.0.0-alpha1) of one Elden Ring mod with seven features. It runs through Seamless
Co-op's launcher or another offline start, never with Easy Anti-Cheat, and never touches your
normal online save. Each feature can be switched off on its own in seekers_compass.ini.

Rest at any Site of Grace to find the mod's menu lines: Loadouts, Camp and Roundtable Hold.
Seeker's Compass joins them once you have bought the compass (see 1).


1. MAP MARKS (Seeker's Compass)
- Marks the weapons, spells, armor and talismans lying in the world on your map.
- You choose what shows: by damage stat (Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Faith, Arcane) and
  by item type. Miner's Bell Bearings have a switch of their own.
- Hover a mark to see the item: a weapon's scaling and requirements, a spell's cost, an armor
  piece's bonuses, a talisman's effect.
- To start: buy the Seeker's Compass from Merchant Kale at the Church of Elleh (100 runes),
  then choose "Seeker's Compass" at a grace. Lines that are on glow.
- Works in the game's languages, with the game's own item names.


2. LOADOUTS
- At a grace, save the gear you are wearing to one of five slots, and equip a saved slot
  again later in one step.
- Loadouts are kept per character.


3. CAMP (Grace Items)
The idea: kill enemies, rest at a grace, and for a while your hits make you stronger.

- Sites of grace give you items when you discover them (graces you found before installing
  count too). A pop-up in the Camp menu tells you what you found.
- Two kinds of item, equipped under Camp at a grace:
    Seats (one equipped)       how long the buff lasts: 15 to 60 minutes, by tier.
    Keepsakes (two equipped)   what the buff does.
- When you rest, the kills since your last rest are banked. For every 5 kills, each equipped
  keepsake can hold one more stack, up to its tier's limit.
- While the buff runs, every hit you land adds a stack (at most one every 0.4 seconds).
  Stacks start to drop when you stop landing hits: after 4 seconds, plus 1 second for every
  10 kills banked, up to 10 seconds. They then drop one per second.
- Being hit does not cost stacks. Dying ends the buff.
- At full stacks your character glows red.
- The five keepsakes:
    Crystal of Fury            2% more damage to enemies per stack.
    Waterskin of Second Wind   stamina recovers faster per stack.
    Pack of the Bulwark        3% less poise damage taken per stack (you are harder to stagger).
    Votive Candle of Warding   at full stacks, a shell cuts the next 3 hits you take by 4% per
                               stack.
    Platter of Leeching        at full stacks, your next hit heals 0.8% of your max HP per stack.
  A used shell or heal comes back when your stacks drop below full and you build them up again.
- Tiers 1 to 5 hold 3, 5, 6, 8 and 10 stacks and are found across the Lands Between. Tier 6
  holds 12 and is found in the Realm of Shadow.
- Your seat and keepsakes appear on the ground beside you while you sit. Only you see them.
- Only enemies are affected: none of this changes damage between players.
- Status lines in Camp show the buff's minutes left and the kills banked at your last rest.


4. AUTO PICKUP
- Plants and crafting materials, items lying in the world, lootable remains and your own lost
  runes are picked up as you walk past, without the button and without the animation.
- Off unless you turn them on: what killed enemies drop, and treasure chests.
- Optional: pause picking up while you are in combat.


5. BELL BEARINGS
- The first time you talk to a merchant, you receive their bell bearing, so you no longer have
  to kill them for the Twin Maiden Husks to sell their stock. The merchant keeps trading.
- Covers the wandering merchants and these named sellers: Gostoc, Corhyn, D, Patches, Sellen,
  Bernahl, Miriel, the Blackguard, Thops and Moore.
- At the Twin Maiden Husks, Purchase opens one shop with the stock of every bell bearing you
  have handed in.


6. ROUNDTABLE HUBS
- After you defeat a story boss, Smithing Master Hewg, the Twin Maiden Husks, Roderika and
  Enia also wait in that boss's room, so you can upgrade, shop, tune spirits and trade
  remembrances without going back to Roundtable Hold. 28 rooms, from Margit to the Realm of
  Shadow, and Redmane Castle after Radahn.
- The rooms you have unlocked are listed under Roundtable Hold in the map's grace list.
- "Roundtable Hold" in the grace menu picks your default room; its icon on the map takes you
  straight there.
- In Roundtable Hold itself the Husks sit beside Hewg.
- The Husks look like a travelling merchant with an instrument.


7. DAMAGE DIAL (off unless you turn it on)
- DamageTaken: how much damage you take. 100 is normal, 50 is half, 200 is double.
- DamageDealt: how much damage your hits do, counted the same way. It also scales stance
  damage, the bleed and frostbite bursts your attacks cause, and your spirit summons' hits.
- It changes only your own damage, also in someone else's game, and not damage between
  players.
- Both can be changed while the game is running.


SETTINGS
ELDEN RING\Game\OfflineMods\SeekersCompass\seekers_compass.ini  (open it with Notepad)
Each feature has a section with Enabled = 1 or 0; restart the game after changing Enabled.
Everything else in the file applies about a second after you save it.


NOT TESTED YET
This is a test build. Known gaps:
- Co-op with a second player has not been tried, with or without the mod on both sides.
- Camp's stacks were checked with test keys, not in a real fight, and the on-screen buff
  icon may not show.
- The tier 6 keepsakes, some hub rooms' character spots, and the single shop at the Husks
  have had little or no play.
If something looks wrong, send seekers_compass.log from the folder above.
